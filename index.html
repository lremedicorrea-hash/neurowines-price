Get an estimated retail price of any given wine based on its Ex Works price, Route to Market and Destination. Make sure to fill in the data below and hit calculate route to market button. You can also do reverse pricing further below.

Available as an installable browser app.
STEP 1 · PRODUCT & ROUTE
The Route to Market changes the estimated retail price. Importer = Importer → Distributor → Retail → Consumer. Direct-to-retail routes remove commercial layers and reduce the estimated retail price.
Please review the highlighted fields before calculating.
Verified regulatory data is separated from commercial estimates. Freight, handling and margins can be edited in Behind the Scenes.
